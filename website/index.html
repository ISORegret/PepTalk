What’s inside

Built for your routine

Log weight and injections, see weekly dose and weight change, estimated medication levels, protocol-style export, and never miss a low vial or due dose.

Summary

Your dashboard: quick log buttons, weight change stats, and goal progress.

  • Log weight & Log injection buttons at the top; weight change stats (total, weekly avg, BMI, % change, to goal) right below
  • Vial low warning banner when less than one dose remains — right where you see it
  • Weight over time graph (wider on mobile); estimated goal date and goal tracker side by side
  • On track? card: typical loss vs yours, time on medication, projected dates to each milestone (achieved ones marked)
  • Week in review, hydration progress, logging streak

Weight

Current weight, BMI, history, and trends.

  • Height for BMI; current weight and BMI cards; link to Calorie / TDEE calculator
  • History list with calendar-style filter — pick a date to see that week’s entries
  • Add, edit, delete entries; data drives Summary and Insights

Injections

Log doses, track vials, rotate sites.

  • Log injection with medication, dose, units, date, and time (morning, midday, evening, night or custom)
  • Side effects and notes; history with week filter so you can focus on a given week
  • Your vials: remaining vs total (mg/ml); injection site rotation grid (green / yellow / red by usage)

Insights

Medication levels, weekly dose table, and per-med summaries.

  • How levels work — short explanation at the top; level phases (Single dose, Building up, Steady state) right under the graph
  • Estimated medication levels over time (half-life model from your logs); Week / Month / 3 mo / All
  • Weekly dose & weight change table: one row per week, columns per medication (doses in the units you logged), total weight change highlighted in the header
  • Active medication cards: current level %, phase, next dose reminder, phase tips; expand for details
  • Side effects from logs and by day in cycle (reference)

Account & cloud backup

Optional sign-in to sync your encrypted data to your Supabase project.

  • When configured, sign in with email — your data stays on the device and is backed up to the cloud
  • Restore on a new phone or resolve local vs cloud when both have data
  • Background sync after saves; clear messages when you’re offline or the network fails

Tools & privacy

Vials, calculators, protocol-style summary, export, reminders. Local-first.

  • Vial inventory and reconstitution calculator (mg, mcg, ml, units, IU); expiry and use-by
  • Protocol-style summary (charts + tables) and clinician PDF from Data; JSON/CSV backup for spreadsheets
  • BMI, TDEE, titration plans; calendar schedules feed the weekly grid in the protocol summary
  • Reminders for injections and weigh-ins; low-vial popup when you open Summary
  • Without cloud setup, everything stays on your device — no selling data
